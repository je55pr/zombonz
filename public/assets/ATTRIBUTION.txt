## CC0 props

The primary prop models in /assets/props are CC0 Poly Haven derivatives. Exact source pages for every prop are recorded in /assets/props/manifest.json.

The wooden-plank geometry was created for Zombonz and uses the CC0 Poly Haven worn_planks material:
https://polyhaven.com/a/worn_planks

Background vehicle conversions are CC0 OpenGameArt assets:
- GAZ-67: https://opengameart.org/content/lowpoly-soviet-jeep
- Soviet military off-road vehicle: https://opengameart.org/content/soviet-military-off-road-vehicle
- T-12 Soviet light tank: https://opengameart.org/content/t-12-soviet-light-tank-lowpoly

Full intake hashes, API snapshots, source archives and conversion notes are kept outside the repository under C:\ChatGPT\Shared\Cache\ZombonzAssets. Repo documentation under docs/assets records the runtime selection and processing state.
